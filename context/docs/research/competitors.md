# Paystack: competitors

Raw research, not conclusions. Checked 2026-11-13. Headlines quoted from each competitor's own homepage where it could be fetched.

Note: Stripe owns Paystack (acquired 2020) and is a founding shareholder of The Stack Group, so it is a parent, not a competitor. Source: https://techpoint.africa/news/paystack-creates-holding-company/

## Pan-African gateways

### Flutterwave
- Headline: "Endless possibilities for all". Subhead: "Empowering businesses of all sizes with seamless payment solutions tailored for enterprises, startups, and emerging markets." Source: https://flutterwave.com/us (checked 2026-11-13)
- Claims: "We accept payments in more than thirty currencies"; "500k+ payments daily"; "15+ payment options". Same source.
- Products: Collect Payments, Send Money, Store, Payment Links, Invoices, Capital, Grow, Card Issuing, Swap, POS, Send App. Same source.
- Third-party framing: operates in more African markets than Paystack (Semafor, https://www.semafor.com/article/01/20/2026/paystack-eyes-growth-beyond-payments-with-restructuring); "the usual pick for cross-border" (search summary of comparison blogs, e.g. https://boldrails.com/blog/best-payment-gateways-nigeria).
- Claim that Flutterwave became a licensed bank in April 2026: from one comparison blog, **not verified**.

### Kora (korahq.com)
- Headline: "The new standard for payments across Africa". Page title: "Pan-African payment infrastructure". Source: https://www.korahq.com/ (checked 2026-11-13)
- Claims: "3x faster conversions on payments. Guaranteed."; "Now Covering 6+ African Countries"; "Built for developers." Same source.
- Products: Pay-ins, Payment Links, Kora Checkout, Virtual Accounts, Card Issuance, Payouts, Bulk and Multicurrency Payouts, Mobile Money, Instant Settlements, KYC/KYB. Same source.

### Peach Payments
- Headline: "The payments platform powering Africa's growth". Self-description: "the leading payment gateway for Africa's most ambitious enterprises"; exists "to solve the fragmentation of African commerce." Source: https://www.peachpayments.com/ (checked 2026-11-13)
- Targets mid-size to enterprise; sectors include ecommerce, education, insurance, betting and gaming, SaaS. Offers "Peach Orchestration". Markets named: South Africa, Mauritius, Kenya; claims 15 markets. Same source.

## Nigeria

### Moniepoint (and Monnify, its web gateway)
- moniepoint.com blocked automated fetch (403). Own headline: Not found.
- Third-party framing: "Nigeria's largest merchant acquirer"; "Africa's all-in-one financial platform for payments, banking, credit and business management". Source: search summary of https://techeconomy.ng/beyond-payments-how-moniepoint-powered-nigerias-msme-growth-in-2025 (not verified verbatim)
- Monnify processed about ₦25 trillion in 2025 (report). Source: https://dabafinance.com/en/news/moniepoint-monnify-processes-payments-in-2025 [search index, not fetched]
- Moniepoint MFB disbursed over ₦1 trillion in SME credit in 2025 (company-reported). Source: https://ng.moniepoint.com/blog/moniepoint-celebrates-a-decade-of-impact-microfinance-bank-disburses-over-naira1-trillion-in-credit-to-empower-small-businesses-in-2025 [search index, not fetched]
- Relevance: Paystack MFB (Jan 2026) moves Paystack toward Moniepoint's bank-plus-payments model. INFERRED.

### Interswitch
- Headline: "Building the Rails of Africa's Digital Future". Self-description: "Africa's leading payments technology company." Source: https://www.interswitchgroup.com/ (checked 2026-11-13)
- Segments: individuals, SMEs ("Smart payment & growth enablers for SMEs"), enterprises, developers and aggregators. Brands: Verve, Quickteller, Payment Gateway (IPG). Countries named: Kenya, Nigeria, Uganda, Ghana ("Coming soon"). Same source.
- Third-party: "one of Nigeria's most established enterprise payment infrastructures". Source: search summary of https://planetweb.ng/paystack-vs-flutterwave-vs-interswitch/

### OPay, Kuda
- Named by Semafor as having loyal consumer and digital-banking bases (relevant to Zap and Paystack MFB, less to merchant payments). Source: https://www.semafor.com/article/01/20/2026/paystack-eyes-growth-beyond-payments-with-restructuring
- OPay described as a USSD and wallet specialist in comparison blogs. Own positioning: Not found.

## Kenya

### Pesapal
- Headline: "Start Accepting M-Pesa and Card Payments." Claims "Regulated by the Central Bank of Kenya." Source: https://www.pesapal.com/ (checked 2026-11-13)

## Price comparison (Nigeria, third-party, conflicting)

- Flutterwave: given as 1.4% + ₦100, 1.8% or 2% depending on source. **Unverified, sources conflict.**
- Moniepoint: given as 1.7% or 1.49% + ₦100, or 1.2% + ₦100. **Unverified, sources conflict.**
