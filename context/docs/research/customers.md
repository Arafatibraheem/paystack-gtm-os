## Testimonials and quotes

**Verbatim quotes could not be verified.** The Wall of Love page (https://paystack.com/wall-of-love) blocks automated fetch. The search index summarised it as follows (paraphrase, not quotes; the indexed copy was flagged as about 1,284 days old, so roughly mid-2023):

- MTN, Olubayo Adekanmbi, Chief Transformation Officer: praises delivery speed and execution; fewer payment failures and more payment options for customers. (paraphrase)
- Reliance HMO, Chief Technical Officer: recurring billing helped them get people to subscribe to affordable health insurance plans. (paraphrase)
- Renmoney, Chief Risk Officer: significant rise in loan collections and faster online onboarding. Attribution conflicts: one Paystack page names "Daniel Watts, Chief Risk Officer". (paraphrase)
- Unnamed merchants: "grew 60%+ in just 6 months"; checkout "has never failed"; integration "under 30 minutes"; a CTO said webhooks "automatically notify us of all successful transactions". (fragments from search index; unverified)

Customer story headlines (search-index titles, reliable as titles):
- "How Paystack streamlined our operations and reclaimed 93% of the processing time"
- "80% increase in payment processing speed since we switched to Paystack Checkout"
- "how chowdeck delivered 1b in sales across 5 countries"

Customer names behind the first two headlines: Not found.

Other quotes found:
- PiggyVest co-founder on early card costs: paying with card "would cost us about 1.5% of whatever the customer is depositing" (search summary of https://techpoint.africa/insight/piggyvest-pocketapp-payment-infrastructure/; not verified verbatim).
