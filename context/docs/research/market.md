# Paystack: market, regulation and urgency

Raw research, not conclusions. Checked 2026-11-13. Most sources are press and vendor blogs, not primary regulator circulars. Verify dates against the regulator before using them in copy.

## Nigeria

### NRS (formerly FIRS) e-invoicing mandate
- Run by the Nigeria Revenue Service through the Merchant Buyer Solution (MBS), called the National E-Invoicing & Electronic Fiscal System. Source: https://www.financialnigeria.com/nrs-sets-july-31-deadline-for-large-taxpayers-to-fully-adopt-e-invoicing-system-news-3042.html (search summary)
- Large taxpayers: deadline 31 July 2026; NRS has begun compliance monitoring. Source: https://allafrica.com/stories/202607210770.html (search summary)
- Medium taxpayers (turnover ₦1bn to ₦5bn): go-live 1 July 2026; enforcement January to March 2027 (per a KPMG Feb 2026 timeline as summarised by a third party). Source: https://sharedserviceslink.com/news/nigeria-enters-next-e-invoicing-phase-on-1st-july (search summary)
- Emerging taxpayers: go-live 1 July 2027. Same source.
- Relevance to Paystack buyers: invoicing and reconciliation tooling. INFERRED.

### CBN POS geo-fencing
- Circular dated 25 August 2025: POS terminals must be geo-tagged and routed through a PTSA (NIBSS or UPSL); agents to operate within 10 metres of a registered location; original deadline 20 October 2025. Source: https://techcabal.com/2025/08/27/cbn-10-metre-rule-pos-agents/ (search summary)
- Later circular: radius widened to 70 metres, enforcement moved to 1 August 2026. Source: https://techcabal.com/2026/05/30/nigerias-central-bank-expands-pos-operating-radius-to-70-metres/ ; https://leadership.ng/cbn-extends-pos-geo-fencing-enforcement-deadline-to-august-2026/
- Earlier reported penalty: minimum ₦5 million plus ₦300,000 per day. Source: https://nairametrics.com/2025/09/24/pos-geo-tagging-fintechs-fear-disruptions-losses-as-cbn-deadline-nears/ (search summary)
- Whether enforcement started on 1 Aug 2026 or was extended again: Not found.

### CBN AML directive (March 2026)
- Requires real-time transaction monitoring, customer risk profiling, sanctions screening, BVN/NIN verification; AI monitoring audited annually. Banks have 18 months, fintechs 24 months; implementation roadmaps due within 3 months. Source: https://techpoint.africa/guide/cbn-fintech-regulations/ (published 2026-05-05)

### Other Nigeria rules (same source unless stated: https://techpoint.africa/guide/cbn-fintech-regulations/)
- Draft APP (authorised push payment) fraud guidelines: report within 24 hours, interbank notice within 30 minutes, refunds within 48 hours or 16 working days. Status: draft.
- ISO 20022: National Payment Stack launched November 2025 on this standard.
- Revised cash policy (2025): individual weekly withdrawal cap ₦500,000 (3% fee above), corporate ₦5m (5% fee). Pushes volume to digital channels. INFERRED.
- NDPA 2023 and GAID 2025 data protection: 72-hour breach notice, DPIAs, cross-border transfer controls.
- Licence capital: Switching & Processing and MMO ₦2bn; PSSP/PTSP ₦100m; PSB ₦5bn. Figures may predate current rules.
- CBN virtual asset rules effective 5 November 2026: ₦1bn to ₦15bn capital for crypto and stablecoin operators; compliance by 30 June 2027. Source: https://techcabal.com/2026/11/06/cbn-sec-divide-crypto-oversight/ (search summary)
- Proposed CBN Regulatory Passporting Programme for mutual licence recognition with Ghana, Kenya, South Africa. Source: https://africa.com/?p=278919 (search summary)

### Betting (a Paystack vertical: "Paystack for Betting")
- Central Gaming Bill rejected by President Tinubu in late 2025 after a 2024 Supreme Court ruling gave states control of lotteries and gaming. Source: https://igamingbusiness.com/legal-compliance/regulation/lagos-regulator-bashir-are-state-cooperation-nigeria/ (search summary)
- Lagos (LSLGA): 5% withholding tax on betting winnings from early 2026; online sports betting licence ₦50,000,000 first year. Sources: https://www.yogonet.com/international/news/2026/02/23/117721-lagos-introduces-5-withholding-tax-on-online-betting-winnings ; https://www.mondaq.com/nigeria/gaming/1662320/navigating-nigerias-gaming-laws-the-start-of-a-new-chapter (search summary)
- Interstate reciprocity licensing framework (FSGRN, May 2025). Source: https://www.vixio.com/insights/gc-nigerian-state-regulators-agree-unified-licensing-process-national-licensing (search summary)

## Kenya

- KRA eTIMS: "No Invoice, No Deduction" rule effective 1 January 2026; turnover threshold removed so all businesses are in scope; automated matching of supplier eTIMS data to buyer VAT claims from 1 January 2026. Source: https://www.fonoa.com/resources/blog/e-invoicing-africa-surprises-kenya-ghana-zimbabwe-nigeria ; https://www.vatupdate.com/2026/01/22/e-invoicing-reforms-accelerate-across-africa-key-developments-and-timelines-as-of-january-2026/ (search summaries)
- Draft National Payment System Policy 2026 published by CBK. Contents relevant to merchants: Not found (not read). Source: https://www.centralbank.go.ke/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Draft-National-Payment-System-Policy-2026.pdf
- PesaLink real-time bank payments (since 2017). Paystack reportedly added Pesalink to checkout in July 2026 (unverified, see [company.md](company.md)). Source: https://www.mastercard.com/global/en/news-and-trends/Insights/2026/real-time-payments-in-africa-unlocking-digital-growth.html

## Ghana

- Bank of Ghana moving to license virtual asset providers. Source: https://vixio.com/insights/pc-five-fintech-and-payments-trends-will-define-africa-2026
- Bank of Ghana merchant payment directives 2025 to 2026: Not found.

## South Africa

- VAT e-invoicing: no mandate in force; full implementation estimated 2028 or later (sources conflict, one says 2030). Source: https://www.fonoa.com/resources/blog/south-africa-e-invoicing-real-time-vat-reporting (search summary)
- SARB March 2026 working paper making the case for open banking; drafting legislation to overhaul the national payments system. Source: https://africa.com/?p=278919 (search summary)
- PayShap instant payments growing. Merchant specifics: Not found. Source: https://cib.absa.africa/home/insights-and-events/instant-payments-and-africas-shifting-regulatory-landscape/

## Pan-African market context

- H1 2026 startup funding (Africa: The Big Deal): Egypt $327m, Nigeria $254m, Kenya $126m, South Africa $83m; Big Four 58% of funding. Continent total about $1.44bn. Source: https://thebigdeal.substack.com/p/h12026c (via search summary of https://african.business/2026/07/innov-africa-deals/egypt-leads-africas-start-up-funding-revival-as-nigeria-regains-momentum)
- Cross-border payments in Africa remain among the most expensive globally. Source: https://www.premiumtimesng.com/business/financial-inclusion/898887-financial-inclusion-policy-fragmentation-dollar-dependence-stall-cross-border-payments-in-africa.html
- Payments firms expected to be treated as systemically important by regulators in Kenya, Ghana, South Africa. Source: https://vixio.com/insights/pc-five-fintech-and-payments-trends-will-define-africa-2026
