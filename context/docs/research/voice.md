## Observed patterns (INFERRED)

- Africa-first framing: "for Africa", "businesses in Africa", "African entrepreneurs" recur in every source above.
- Simple, plain claims: "simple", "easy", "fast", "secure", "fair". Short benefit-led bullets.
- Developer speed as proof: "Start accepting payments in just 30 minutes."
- Aligned to merchant success: "only makes money when you do".
- Naming: products are named plainly with the brand in front (Paystack Terminal, Paystack Checkout, Zap by Paystack, Paystack MFB).
