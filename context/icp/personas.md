# Paystack buyer personas

Built only from [context/docs/research/](../docs/research/) (checked 2026-11-13). Sources key as in [icp.md](icp.md): CO, CU, CP, MK, SG, VO.

**Quote warning:** the research has almost no verified buyer quotes. The Wall of Love could not be fetched, so testimonial lines below are paraphrases from search results, labelled as such. Do not use them as quotes in copy until checked on https://paystack.com/wall-of-love.

## 1. Champion: technical lead

| Field | Detail | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Job titles | CTO, Chief Technical Officer, IT Manager | CU |
| Example | Reliance HMO CTO; Renmoney IT Manager (Adija Uzodinma); unnamed CTO on Wall of Love | CU |
| Measured on | Open question. Outcomes these buyers credit Paystack for: integration speed, checkout reliability, automated transaction notifications | CU |
| Pain 1 | Payments that fail at checkout. Paraphrase: their checkout "has never failed" (unnamed reviewer) | CU |
| Pain 2 | Slow integration. Paraphrase: integration took "under 30 minutes" (unnamed reviewer) | CU |
| Pain 3 | Manual tracking of successful payments. Fragment: webhooks "automatically notify us of all successful transactions" (unnamed CTO) | CU |
| Starts looking when | Building a new product or recurring billing; payments slow or failing; launching in a new market (INFERRED) | SG |
| Objections | Open question. None voiced by technical buyers in research | n/a |

## 2. Economic buyer: founder or business executive

| Field | Detail | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Job titles | Founder / co-founder (startups); Chief Transformation Officer (enterprise) | CU |
| Example | PiggyVest co-founder; MTN, Olubayo Adekanmbi, Chief Transformation Officer | CU |
| Measured on | Open question. Outcomes credited to Paystack: fewer payment failures, more payment options, delivery speed | CU |
| Pain 1 | Cost of card payments. PiggyVest co-founder: paying with card "would cost us about 1.5% of whatever the customer is depositing" (verbatim not verified) | CU |
| Pain 2 | Payment failures and too few payment options. Paraphrase of MTN testimonial | CU |
| Pain 3 | Slow vendor delivery. Paraphrase: MTN praises "delivery speed and execution" | CU |
| Starts looking when | Raising funding, entering a new market, or customers asking for more services (Akinlade, Jan 2026) | SG, CO |
| Objections | Price versus competitors (third-party fee comparisons conflict); need for wider market coverage (Flutterwave); need for banking and credit alongside payments (Moniepoint) | CP, CO |

## 3. Influencer: risk and compliance

| Field | Detail | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Job titles | Chief Risk Officer | CU |
| Example | Renmoney Chief Risk Officer (one page names Daniel Watts; attribution conflicts) | CU |
| Measured on | Open question. Outcomes credited to Paystack: loan collections, onboarding speed | CU |
| Pain 1 | Weak loan collections. Paraphrase: "significant rise in loan collections" after Paystack | CU |
| Pain 2 | Slow customer onboarding. Paraphrase: faster online onboarding | CU |
| Pain 3 | Open question. Fraud and reconciliation are Paystack claims ("advanced fraud detection", "reporting for accounting, reconciliation, and audits"), not buyer words | VO |
| Starts looking when | Compliance deadlines: CBN AML directive (fintechs, 24 months from Mar 2026), Nigeria e-invoicing, Kenya eTIMS (INFERRED link) | MK, SG |
| Objections | Held funds and pending payouts (recurring Trustpilot complaints) | CU |
